Method, definitions and caveats (secondary — kept out of the first read)

What is counted

One row is one activity report (5Ws): one session, at one place, on one day, with its attendance. Attendances are sessions, not unique people — one person at three sessions is three attendances, so attendances are never added to any count of people. No denominator is implied anywhere except coverage against the roster, which is stated as such.

Non-additivity

Activity families, target groups and IASC layers overlap and a record may appear in more than one, so their rows are not additive. Only the total report count is a sum.

Small cells

A count below 5 reads “fewer than 5”, and no figure smaller than a palika is shown, so no cell can point to an individual. The floor is printed on the page, not hidden in a note.

Source, date and correction status

Source, the data date and the capture state travel with every block. A record carrying a correction is flagged rather than silently rewritten. This fixture is synthetic and settles no institutional question.

What this cannot show

No trend, no target achievement, no coverage gap as a proportion of need: this data carries no MHPSS-specific denominator. Where a ratio would need one, the concept says it cannot be calculated instead of printing a number.
