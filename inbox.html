Layer 2 · where the forms arrive

Where the forms come in

A field worker fills a form on their phone, exports a file, and sends it to you. You drop the file here and it becomes a table. This is the Layer 2 end of the one-direction boundary: field → coordination, never back.

This is carried by hand, not synced. There is no server behind this site, so nothing travels from a phone to here on its own — someone sends the file over WhatsApp or email and someone drops it in. That is enough to run a trial and to prove the questions work. It is not enough to run a response: a live system needs the WHO or EDCD backend, and then this page becomes a view of a database instead of a pile of files.
