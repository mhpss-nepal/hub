Two organisations at one site on one day is a flag, not a finding. It is often legitimate — a counselling session and a child-friendly space in the same shelter reach different people. It becomes double counting only when the same people are counted twice, and only the two organisations can settle that. The same organisation appearing twice under different funding tags is a funding attribution, not duplication, and is shown as such.

Who is present where, doing what — the 4Ws proper, at palika level. Demonstration mode, until the review by WHO and the Ministry. Everything the coverage view computes is published as it stands — site level, organisation names, no small-cell suppression — so the full behaviour of the system can be judged. Two things are never published: the free-text description of a report, and any focal-point contact (which never reaches the register at all). After the review, what is public and what is not is decided and the rules are tightened accordingly.
