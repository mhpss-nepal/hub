MHPSS Nepal · coordination dashboard

First compilation, unconfirmed. Built on 19 November 2026 from two sources: the CMC-Nepal flood-response staff list and the EDCD mental health activity workbook (activity log, activity plan and workforce sheets). One row is one organisation in one palika. Every row stays unconfirmed until the organisation confirms or corrects it with the Referral Directory form. Staff names and phone numbers are kept in a separate restricted list and are not on this page.

Who is where

A dot means the organisation appears in that palika in at least one source. Hover or tap a dot for the services recorded. "District level" collects records that name the district but no palika.

Gaps by palika

Number of organisations recording each service in each palika. GBV case management, child protection case management, legal services and safe shelter do not appear in either source; they are listed so the gap stays visible until partners add them through the form.

Organisations by palika

Helplines named in the EDCD workbook

As written in the helpline sheet of the EDCD workbook. Each number is to be checked with its operator before it is shared outside coordination.

Map

Neither source holds coordinates. Pins appear here when an organisation confirms a service point with the Referral Directory form, which records the exact place and its pin.

Service groups are taken from the activity descriptions in the source files. Dates are the latest Bikram Sambat date written in the activity log; dates written in AD or later than Mangsir 2083 were left out as probable entry errors.
