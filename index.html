A 5Ws is not a list of who is present. It is the instrument that says which sites are at zero. The denominator is the official holding-centre roster; a site that appears in reports but was never on the roster is shown separately and is never counted as a gap.

Coverage of the holding-centre roster

Every site on the official roster, whether or not anyone has reported working there. Ordered by district, then by roster number. Hover a site for its detail.
No service reported — a gap Reported on 3 days or fewer Reported on more than 3 days Roster population shown where recorded

Who is doing what, where

Reach by organisation. One measure, one colour — the bars are ranked, not categorised, so colour carries no extra meaning here.

Contacts per day

Bikram Sambat dates as reported. No calendar conversion is performed anywhere in this system — no verified BS conversion table has been obtained.

Activity mix by district

Six bars per district, side by side and always in the same order, so every family is measured from the axis rather than off the top of whatever is beneath it. They are shaded in one colour from palest to darkest by how specialised the support is — the same ordering as the IASC intervention pyramid on the public page. Six separate hues could not be made colour-blind-safe at six slots; lightness is the one channel every kind of colour vision keeps. Figures sit above the bars because the smallest are a few pixels tall, and the strip under each group is the same six normalised to 100%, so a district with 185 contacts still shows its composition at full length.

Deployed workforce

One row per person, coded cadre. In the live system this replaces six overlapping name lists. No contact details are held here — the hub records that a cadre is deployed at a district, not how to reach an individual.

Helpline demand

Remote contacts belong in the same count as face-to-face ones. A line reporting nothing is shown as reporting nothing, not omitted — the silence is the finding.

This is a demonstration. Every number, name, site figure and date on this page is synthetic, generated deterministically on 15 September 2026 so that the design can be reviewed without exposing response data. It is not the response's data and must not be quoted, screenshotted as evidence, or cited in any report.

What is real here is the architecture, the instrument, the controlled vocabularies and the method — the site and activity lists are drawn from records the response already holds, and are marked draft until EDCD and the MHPSS Technical Working Group sign them off.

No personal data. This page holds no names, telephone numbers or email addresses of service users or of staff. The workforce view records cadre and district only. The system is a coordination system: it holds service-level information and does not hold individual-level information.

Governance. Three questions raised on 14 September 2026 — hosting on a non-WHO account, AI assistance in development, and the procurement route for a domain — remain open. Until each has a written answer this remains a demonstration running on synthetic content only.

No WHO or Government of Nepal emblem is used: WHO emblem use requires express written permission and the Nepal national emblem belongs to the Ministry.
